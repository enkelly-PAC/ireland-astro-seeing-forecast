    How the seeing score works
    Selected imaging targetMoon

    The planner's selected target is shown with qualitative atmospheric blur and image motion at representative seeing scores. Telescope aperture, magnification, wavelength and local turbulence will change the real view. Good seeing is shown as mostly clear with occasional softening, rather than constant image drift. The simulator labels use the same approximate Pickering-style arcsecond conversion as the hourly seeing score.
